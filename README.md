# Packaging Material Index-Price Audit

Recalculate indexed packaging prices and recover formula, lag, conversion, freight, and volume errors.

**Primary buyer:** Consumer goods manufacturers and packaging buyers. **Evidence:** supplier agreements, packaging specifications, resin paper and metal indexes, formula lags, conversion costs, freight, volumes, invoices, credits, and rebates.

Full local application built with React, Vite, Express, PostgreSQL, and OpenRouter. Includes 15 domain-specific capabilities, 105 custom AI workbench fields, three scenario-fill controls per feature, operational registers, workflow transitions, analytics, professional AI decision briefs, audit history, and at least 15 PostgreSQL records per capability.

## Domain capabilities

- Packaging agreement library
- SKU specification registry
- Commodity index ingestion
- Index publication validation
- Formula lag calculation
- Conversion cost validation
- Freight component audit
- Volume tier calculation
- Lightweighting adjustment
- Scrap factor control
- Price effective-date check
- Invoice recalculation
- Supplier dispute workflow
- Credit reconciliation
- Material supplier analytics

Run `./start.sh`, then open <http://127.0.0.1:4704>. API: `5704`.
